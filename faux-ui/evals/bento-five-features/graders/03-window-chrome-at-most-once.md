Window chrome (a title bar with traffic-light dots, a browser bar, or a full app frame with sidebar and header) appears in at most one tile, and no tile's edge slices through a real word or a wordmark: a chip, name, or label that reaches the tile edge is either whole or cut cleanly outside its text. FAIL if two or more tiles each show a framed window, or if any real word is cut mid-letter by a tile edge.
