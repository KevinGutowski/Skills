Construction drawing for an empty state

Cause: no data in range. Relationship: records sit on one time axis at a shared pitch, and the selected range covers positions that nothing occupies. The absence is drawn as the gap it leaves in something regular, not as an icon.

1. The full drawing, 560 × 440

For a first-use page, a 404 or a docs figure. Shown at native size; on a narrow screen the panel scrolls sideways instead of shrinking the labels. One ink (currentColor), 1-unit strokes, transparent forms, one translucent highlight on the named range. Opacity carries the hierarchy: 1 for records and axis, 0.32 for dimensions and absent outlines. The note is in words, not a zero count.

2. The compact version, in the component

Derived from the same geometry: text, dimensions and ticks removed, the viewBox cropped to the kept forms plus 16 units, vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke" on every stroke, and the absent dash shortened to 2 2. Nothing is rescaled or redrawn. The frame (title and range) stays; the card size drops the visual. The action is a static specimen, not a working button.
