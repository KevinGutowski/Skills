# Sources

**Sources** — this skill aggregates two Apple Human Interface talks on UX writing:
- *Apple WWDC 2024, session 10140 — "Add personality to your app through UX writing" (Alex & Liv, Human Interface design team). https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10140/*
- *Apple WWDC 2022, session 10037 — "Writing for interfaces" (Kaely Coon & Jennifer Bush). https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/10037/*
- *Apple WWDC 2025, session 404 — "Make a big impact with small writing changes" (Liv Huntley & Jennifer Bush). https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/404/*
- *Apple WWDC 2017 session 819 — "Designing for a Global Audience" (Sarah Harling) (lost session, via WWDC Index archive) — the localization-ready-writing layer.*
- *Michael J. Metts & Andy Welfle, "Writing Is Designing" (Rosenfeld Media, 2020) — channel-switcher tone, tone profiles, voice documentation, inclusive language, clarity craft. Book-depth companion to Welfle's Config-2025 layer below.*
- *Aarron Walter, "Designing for Emotion" (A Book Apart, 2011) — design persona, surface taper, delight-layer rules, defending the voice.*
- *Leon's tasteskill 2.5.1 (`design-taste-frontend`, https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill, MIT, rev `f6b7657`, 2026-11-07): its copy, copy-voice, copy-registers, and copy-moves references, paraphrased into [marketing-page-copy.md](marketing-page-copy.md). Its copy-tells reference is folded in `frontend-design` (anti-slop-catalog).*
- *Vercel, Writing Guidelines (https://github.com/vercel-labs/writing-guidelines, MIT, rev `83e2316`, 2026-06-01), wrapped as `writing-guidelines` in https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills rev `5a37a63`: only the unit-format convention and the rhetorical-question conflict were used here. The rest is docs prose.*
