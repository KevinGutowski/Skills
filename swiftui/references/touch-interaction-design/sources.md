# Sources

**Sources** — this skill aggregates two Apple WWDC 2018 sessions:
- *Session 804 — "The Life of a Button" (Julian, designer/prototyper; Hugo Verweij's sound half lives in [../sound-design.md](../sound-design.md)). https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/804/*
- *Session 803 — "Designing Fluid Interfaces" (Chan Karunamuni, Nathan de Vries, Marcos Alonso — the iPhone X gesture team). https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/803/*
- *MDS (Matt D. Smith, Shift Nudge) — live design sessions, YouTube.*
- *Emil Kowalski, `apple-design` skill (https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills at `715c180`, MIT) — paraphrased deltas in fluid-interfaces only: the deceleration rates as numbers, velocity sign deciding the release, and the rubber-band formula. Its `0.55` constant is a community measurement of iOS scroll views, not an Apple figure. The rest of that skill was not folded; see `docs/emil-kowalski-skills-source-mining-2026-11.md`.*
