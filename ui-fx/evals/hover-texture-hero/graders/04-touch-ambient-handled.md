The answer says that on phones (screens without hover) the texture plays by itself while on screen, and handles that so automatic motion stops within five seconds or has a visible control: for example `data-motion="hover"` (any value other than `ambient`) on an ancestor, rendering it `static` on touch, or a visible pause control. It does not add its own reduced-motion guard that replaces or removes the one in the installed file.
